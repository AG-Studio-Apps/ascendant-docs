## 2. What Ascendant is, and what it is not

Ascendant calculates the positions of the Sun, Moon and planets for a moment and a place, and presents the interpretations that the Western astrological tradition attaches to those positions.

The calculations are astronomy and they are accurate. The interpretations are astrology. They are presented because they are what the tradition holds, not as findings about you, about anyone else, or about what will happen.

**The App is for interest and reflection. It is not advice.** Nothing in it is medical, psychological, legal, financial, or relationship advice, and nothing in it should be relied on when making a decision of that kind. It does not predict events, and it is written throughout not to try. If you are making a decision that matters, consult someone qualified to help with it.

Where the App compares two charts, it describes what the tradition says about how the two sets of positions relate. It is not an assessment of two people, of their relationship, or of whether they should have one.
