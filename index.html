Yours to the minute

Born the same day as someone else and your charts still differ. The Moon moves a degree every two hours, and the sign rising on the horizon turns a degree every four minutes.

So the sky is cast for your minute and your place, never for your date. Positions come from a vendored copy of the cosinekitty/astronomy library and agree with Swiss Ephemeris to a fraction of an arcsecond.

And how two charts meet

Set your chart beside someone else's and see what draws the two of you together and what pulls, contact by contact, in the terms astrologers have used for centuries.

It says whether a pairing is easy, demanding or evenly matched, and it counts the contacts the tradition names as deciding one. It will not give you a percentage, because there is no honest arithmetic behind one.

It stays on your phone

There is no account and no server of ours. Your birth details are stored on the device and the chart is worked out there. Nothing about it is uploaded, and there is nothing to delete anywhere but the app.

The one exception is typing a place: the name you type goes to Apple's geocoding service to be turned into coordinates, the same way any Maps search does. Your date, your time and your chart do not.
