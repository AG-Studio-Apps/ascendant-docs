## 1. What we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store your birth details, your charts, your readings, or anything you do in the app. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no profile.

Ascendant contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting service and no third-party software development kits. We do not know how many charts you have cast, what they say, or whether you have opened the app at all.

## 2. What is stored on your device

Your birth details, any other charts you save, your chosen house system and your chosen observing location are stored on the device using the operating system's own storage. They stay there. They are included in your device backup if you have one, which is between you and Apple.

Deleting a chart in the app deletes it. Deleting the app removes all of it.

## 3. Searching for a place

When you type a place into the birth details or the location picker, that text is sent to Apple's geocoding service to be turned into coordinates and a timezone, in the same way as a search in Maps. This is the only part of casting a chart that is not purely on your device.

What is sent is the text you typed. Your date of birth, your time of birth, your chart and your readings are not sent, and Apple is not told what the place is for.

Apple's handling of that request is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/uk/legal/privacy/).

## 4. Using your current location

If you choose "use my location", the app asks the operating system for a single position and sends it to Apple's geocoding service to get a place name and a timezone. This is optional, it is never on by default, and the app asks for permission the first time.

We do not receive your location. It is not stored anywhere but on your device, and you can revoke the permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Location affects only the houses and the rising sign. Every planetary position is the same wherever you are.

## 5. Buying the app's extra scope

The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account, or your name. What the app receives from Apple is a yes or no to the question of whether the purchase has been made.

## 6. Diagnostics

Test builds distributed through TestFlight include a diagnostics screen that writes a text file of chart calculations and timings to the device. It exists so problems can be reported with evidence. It is not present in the App Store version, nothing is sent automatically, and a report reaches us only if you choose to send it.

## 7. Children

Ascendant is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone. Since we collect nothing from anyone at all, this is a statement of design rather than a policy.

## 8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over personal data held about you.

We hold no personal data about you, so in practice there is nothing for us to give you, correct or erase. Everything is on your device and under your control. If you believe we hold something about you, write to us at the address below and we will look into it, and you may complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/).
